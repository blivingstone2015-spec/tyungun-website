The site

Drive straight in. Then disappear.

A spacious, easy-going site with its own gate leading straight off Flying Fox Road — no four-wheel-drive, no fuss. You can bring a low caravan right in and set up in minutes.

And yet, the moment the gate closes, it feels a world away: completely secluded, regularly mown, and edged by the creek’s finest swimming hole. The only interruptions are curious cattle wandering down for a look, and the occasional wallaby at dusk.

Our most accessible site and the largest gathering of friends — flat, grassy, deeply shaded by the creek, with a big fire pit waiting.

Good to know

01

Nomadic camping

There are no toilets, showers or drinking water on the land — this site is for self-contained campers. Bring your own water and a way to go.

02

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome and Bryce drops a complimentary crate of wood to your fire pit. Total fire bans are observed.

03

Pets & cattle

Dogs are encouraged but this is a working farm — keep them close and under control around the cattle at all times.
