A day at Tyungun

Wash your face in the creek to start the day. Swim at lunch. Nap in the shade of the silky oaks. Sit by the fire as the stars come out — and let the world keep slowing down.

01

The swimming holes

Flying Fox Creek runs clear and cold over the rocks, gathering into crystal swimming holes at every site. Wade in to cool off, float a lazy afternoon away, or just sit in the shallows and listen to the water.

02

Paddle the Coomera

At Twin Pines the river slows and widens — bring a canoe or paddle board, watch for turtles, and drift.

03

Wander the hills

Beautiful grazing country to explore on foot — rolling hills, pine stands, and views that make it hard to believe the coast is forty minutes away.

04

Meet the locals

This is a working cattle farm, and the herd is famously friendly — expect curious visits at the fence line. At dusk the wallabies come out, and an azure kingfisher works the rock pools. Keep dogs close around the stock.

05

Then, the fire.
Then, the stars.

Campfires are welcome at every site, and Bryce drops a complimentary crate of firewood to your pit. When the fire burns low, look up — with no town glow for miles, the night sky here puts on a spectacular show.
