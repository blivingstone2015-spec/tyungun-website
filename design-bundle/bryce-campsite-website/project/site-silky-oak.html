The site

Across the creek, into the quiet.

A large, level flat cradled between the creek and the hills, reached only by crossing the creek some 250 metres off the main road. The crossing is the price of admission — and it buys you total seclusion.

This is cattle country at its most companionable; the herd counts this flat among its favourite spots and tends to come and say hello. With room to spread out and a private swimming hole of its own, it’s the one we point larger groups toward.

Best suited to smaller off-road vans and tents. The creek crossing keeps the casual passer-by out — you’ll have the flat entirely to yourselves.

Good to know

01

Nomadic camping

There are no toilets, showers or drinking water on the land — this site is for self-contained campers. Bring your own water and a way to go.

02

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome and Bryce drops a complimentary crate of wood to your fire pit. Total fire bans are observed.

03

Pets & cattle

Dogs are encouraged but this is a working farm — keep them close and under control around the cattle at all times.
