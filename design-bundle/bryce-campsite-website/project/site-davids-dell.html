The site

A fern-floored room of your own.

Tucked right beside the running creek and its string of natural rock pools, David’s Dell is the most intimate site on the property. A double gate makes setting up a caravan or tent effortless.

Inside, a secluded canopy of trees and a soft floor of ferns wrap the site in complete privacy — you’d never know the quiet road was there. Made for small groups, couples, and the solo camper who wants nothing but birdsong.

Wallabies at dawn, an azure kingfisher over the pools, and rock pools cool enough to swim. The most private of the four for two.

Good to know

01

Nomadic camping

There are no toilets, showers or drinking water on the land — this site is for self-contained campers. Bring your own water and a way to go.

02

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome and Bryce drops a complimentary crate of wood to your fire pit. Total fire bans are observed.

03

Pets & cattle

Dogs are encouraged but this is a working farm — keep them close and under control around the cattle at all times.
