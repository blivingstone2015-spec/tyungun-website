Tyungun is a family-owned cattle property in the rolling hills of Ferny Glen — grazing country threaded by a clear, spring-fed creek and edged by stands of silky oak and pine.

We keep only four sites across the whole 350 acres, each one tucked into its own fold of the land. Arrive, close the gate behind you, and the place is yours.
