Tyungun is a family-owned cattle property in the rolling hills of Ferny Glen, threaded by the clear, spring-fed waters of Flying Fox Creek and edged by stands of silky oak and pine.

It is a real farm — the cattle graze, the seasons turn, and the days keep a slower clock. We open just four sites to guests, scattered across the whole property so that no two parties ever cross paths.

What we offer isn’t an amenity or a facility. It’s space, and the quiet that fills it.

Why it feels different

You won’t see
another soul.

Most campgrounds sell you a numbered plot an arm’s length from a stranger. Tyungun is the opposite. Each site sits in its own fold of the land — behind its own gate, beyond sight and earshot of the others.

Guests tell us the same thing again and again: that they couldn’t see or hear another person the entire stay. That the world slowed the moment they arrived. That they extended a night because they simply didn’t want to leave.

It is, as one put it, “a little piece of paradise — like you’re the only one around for miles.”
