Guest Guide & House Rules

A few things,
before you arrive.

Tyungun is a working farm and a wild, self-contained camp. A little preparation is the difference between a good stay and an unforgettable one — here’s all of it.

01

Arrival & access

The precise address and gate instructions are sent once your booking is confirmed — to protect the farm’s privacy, they aren’t published. Reception is limited on the property, so download your directions before you leave Canungra.

Each site has its own gate. The Swimming Hole, David’s Dell and Twin Pines suit most vehicles; Silky Oak Flat is reached by a creek crossing and needs a 4WD. Please always leave gates as you found them — there are cattle about.

02

Self-contained camping

There are no toilets, no showers and no drinking water anywhere on the land. This is what keeps the sites wild and completely private — and it means you’ll need to be fully self-sufficient.

Bring all your own drinking and washing water, and a way to go to the toilet responsibly (a portable loo, or be prepared to dig well away from the creek). Best suited to caravans, camper-trailers and well-prepared tent campers.

03

What to pack

  • Drinking & washing water (plenty)
  • Portable toilet or trowel
  • All food & a cooler
  • Rubbish bags — carry out all you bring in
  • Firelighters & matches
  • Camp chairs & shade
  • Bathers & a towel for the creek
  • Offline maps & directions
04

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome at every site, in the provided fire pit only. Bryce drops a complimentary crate of firewood to your site — one of the small kindnesses guests mention most.

Never leave a fire unattended, drown it cold before you sleep or leave, and check for total fire bans, which are observed without exception during the warmer months.

05

Pets & cattle

Dogs are warmly encouraged — this is wonderful country for them. But it is an active cattle farm: keep your dog under control and close to camp at all times, and never let them chase or worry stock.

The cattle themselves are friendly and curious, and may wander over to say hello. Enjoy their company, keep a respectful distance, and don’t feed them.

06

Privacy & quiet

The whole point of Tyungun is that you won’t see or hear another soul. Please honour that for your neighbours across the farm — keep music low, generators considerate, and let the sound of the creek be the loudest thing here.

07

Leave no trace

Carry out everything you carry in, including all rubbish. Keep soaps and washing well away from the creek and rock pools so the water stays as clear for the next guest as it was for you. Leave your site exactly as you’d wish to find it.
