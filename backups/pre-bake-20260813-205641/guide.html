Guest Guide & House Rules

A few things,
before you arrive.

Tyungun is a working farm and a self-contained camp. Here’s what to know.

The guide

01

Arrival & access

The precise address and gate instructions are sent once your booking is confirmed.

Each site has its own gate. The Swimming Hole, David’s Dell and Twin Pines suit most vehicles; Silky Oak Flat is reached by a creek crossing and needs a 4WD.

02

Self-contained camping

There are no toilets, no showers and no drinking water anywhere on the land.

Bring your own drinking and washing water, plus a way to go to the toilet responsibly.

03

What to pack

  • Drinking & washing water
  • Portable toilet or trowel
  • All food & a cooler
  • Rubbish bags — carry out all you bring in
  • Firelighters & matches
  • Camp chairs & shade
  • Bathers & a towel for the creek
  • Offline maps & directions
04

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome in the provided fire pits. Bryce drops a complimentary crate of firewood to your site.

Never leave a fire unattended, drown it cold before you sleep or leave, and check for total fire bans during the warmer months.

05

Pets & cattle

Dogs are welcome, but this is an active cattle farm: keep them under control and close to camp.

The cattle are friendly and curious. Keep a respectful distance, and don’t feed them.

06

Privacy & quiet

The whole point of Tyungun is that you won’t see or hear another soul. Keep music low and generators considerate.

07

Leave no trace

Carry out everything you carry in, including all rubbish. Keep soaps and washing well away from the creek and rock pools. Leave your site as you found it.
