The site

Where the river does the talking.

Our riverside site, set on the Coomera among the pines — one of the larger sites and a favourite for swimming, paddling and watching turtles drift in the shallows. A pull-through track makes van set-up simple.

Accessible to most vehicles, weather permitting, and completely private. Fall asleep to running water; wake to a spectacular show of stars with nothing to dim them.

Bring the canoe or paddle board. The Coomera here is clear and slow, perfect for a lazy float or just sitting in the shallows soaking up the serenity.

Good to know

01

Nomadic camping

There are no toilets, showers or drinking water on the land — this site is for self-contained campers. Bring your own water and a way to go.

02

Fires & firewood

Campfires are welcome and Bryce drops a complimentary crate of wood to your fire pit. Total fire bans are observed.

03

Pets & cattle

Dogs are encouraged but this is a working farm — keep them close and under control around the cattle at all times.
