What Makes Tyungun Different

Tyungun Country Retreat is a family-owned, 350-acre cattle farm offering four ultra-private, self-contained camping sites in the rolling hills of Ferny Glen, Queensland. Unlike crowded campgrounds or resort-style farm stays, Tyungun provides true seclusion: each site is tucked into its own fold of the land, completely out of sight of neighbours. When you arrive and close the gate behind you, the entire property is yours alone.

Self-contained camping means total independence. You bring your own water, manage your own sanitation, cook your own meals, and power your own needs. There are no shared facilities, no neighbours' generators, no queues for showers. Just you, the farm, the creek, and 350 acres of cattle country threaded by Flying Fox Creek.

Each of our four sites—The Swimming Hole, Silky Oak Flat, David's Dell, and Twin Pines—offers a distinct experience. Sleep anywhere from 9 to 24 people depending on the site. Access ranges from easy (no 4WD needed) to 4WD-only for the more remote locations. Every site features a private swimming hole, campfire, shade, and a locked gate for complete privacy.
